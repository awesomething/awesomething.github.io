## Solution 2: Four New Site Plans for New Builds

**The outcome:** Four plan concepts ready — not blank pages, not vague sketches — ready to hand to a drafter or submit for preliminary review.

**How it works:**

For each build, you share:
- Lot dimensions / survey (or rough sketch)
- What you're building (bedrooms, baths, footprint size, garage, etc.)
- Any known zoning requirements (setbacks, lot coverage, FAR)

AI produces:

1. **Floor plan concept** via **[Drafted.ai](https://www.drafted.ai)** — generates floor plans, 3D models, and exports to CAD/BIM format. Designed specifically for builders and developers. You pick what works, discard what doesn't.

2. **Site placement brief** — a written document explaining where the building sits on the lot, how setbacks are handled, driveway and utility access, and what the city will likely look at. This becomes the brief for your drafter.

3. **Preliminary checklist per project** — zoning questions to confirm, items that typically trigger city comments, and what to have ready before submission.

**For all four projects in parallel.** Not sequentially. Not one at a time while you wait.

## What This Is Not

To be direct:

- These plans are **not permit-ready construction documents** on their own. You still need a licensed professional to stamp and sign before final city submission.
- These tools produce **floor plans**, not complete engineering site plans. Grading, drainage calculations, and utility connections still need a site engineer if required by your city.
- This is a **workflow accelerator**, not a replacement for your drafter long-term. When they're back, they'll have better briefs, clearer concepts, and less rework.

What it *does* replace: the waiting, the blank-page starting point, and the dependency on one person's availability.
